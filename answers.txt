Question A:
Agile Pros: You get working software every sprint; requirements can change without restarting
Agile Cons: Hard to fix a deadline or budget upfront; needs constant team and consumer availability
Non-Agile Pros: Predictable schedule; full documentation
Non-Agile Cons: Problems only show up at the end; changes are expensive

Question B:
Scrum Master: Facilitates cerimonies, removes blockers, coaches the team on Scrum practice, sheilds the team from mid-scope changes.
Product Owner: Owns and prioritizes the backlog, writes and clarifies user stories and acceptance criteria, represents the customer, accepts or rejects completed work.
